# Course Helper

Course Helper provides local hints and optional assistance with supported course activities.

## Language

**Auto**:
The explicitly started mode that works through supported course activities and continues through the course when its completion requirements are met.

**Course navigation**:
Movement through course pages and activities, including returning to an incomplete lesson. It is distinct from final assessment submission.

**Question capture**:
Observation and local retention of question data supplied by the course for hints and assistance. It is distinct from adding presentation hints to the course's response.
